Figure 1 (main manuscript): Two-panel figure for the delta=0 reference slice. Panel (a) plots the elementary spin-1 protected response curve with the spin-1/2 origin, the magnetic blind point at g=0, the charge blind point at g=1, the closest approach near g=0.590, and the Yang--Mills point g=2 marked. Panel (b) plots magnetic-only, charge-only, and combined uniform protected-response margins versus the half-width R of K_R=[2-R,2+R]. The charge-only margin reaches zero at R=1 and the magnetic-only margin at R=2, while the combined margin remains positive.

Figure 2 (main manuscript): Two-panel bandwidth--smoothness grid on the delta=0 reference slice. Panel (a) classifies 99 tested points as whole-class certified, explicit overlap, or unresolved; the only unresolved points are (sigma_k/m,L)=(0.08,24) and (0.10,16). Panel (b) shows the whole-class unit-covariance lower bound D_L(I) on the same grid on a logarithmic scale; cells with zero lower enclosure are inconclusive.
